DECLINE-CURVE ANALYTICS, MULTI-AGENT AI & GENIE FOR UPSTREAM PRODUCTION

Production Optimizer fuses live well telemetry, decline-curve forecasting, what-if simulation, and a multi-agent supervisor with Databricks Genie to close the gap between forecasted and actual oil production — well by well, hour by hour.

◆ Business Value
  • Close the forecast gap — quantify deviation from decline-curve baselines and act before barrels are lost
  • Optimize per-well intervention — choke, ESP frequency, and gas-lift sliders with simulated NPV impact
  • Natural-language exploration — Ask Genie answers ad-hoc questions over Unity Catalog production tables
  • Multi-agent orchestration — Supervisor agent routes between monitoring, optimization, and recommendation specialists
  • One pane of glass — geospatial field map, digital twin, deep-dive analytics, and AI advisors in a single app
◆ Challenges Addressed
  • Forecast vs. actual blindness — decline curves live in spreadsheets, decoupled from live SCADA
  • Slow what-if cycles — engineers can't simulate choke / lift changes without re-running offline models
  • SQL gatekeeping — operations leaders wait days for production-data answers from analysts
  • Recommendation overload — multiple ML models surface alerts with no prioritization or business context
  • Disconnected analytics — map, twin, and KPI dashboards live in separate, non-interactive tools
